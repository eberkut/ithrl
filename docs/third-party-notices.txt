Documentation site: third-party notices
======================================

These notices cover the theme, fonts and browser assets distributed with the
ithrl documentation site. They do not change ithrl's Apache-2.0 license.

The site uses mkdocs-shadcn 0.12.2, with its bundled CSS, JavaScript, icons
and unmodified fonts. Optional charting, mathematics and live-editing assets
are excluded from the site. MkDocs supplies the browser search assets.
